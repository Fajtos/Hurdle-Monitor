# Protocol H. Panel monitoring with false discovery control

Decided 2026-11-09, after Parts E, F and G and before any run of `application/15_panel_trajectories.R` or `application/16_panel_ebh.R`. The protocol and both scripts are committed and tagged `protocol-H-frozen` before the first run, including the smoke test. This file does not change the earlier protocols.

## Question

A drug safety monitor watches many drug and reaction series at once. A separate alarm rule at level 0.05 for each series does not control the proportion of false discoveries among the series flagged. This part asks whether e-BH applied to the e-detector statistics of the series controls that proportion at the first day on which it flags anything, and what it costs in detection, when the series are real and the shifts are injected.

## Statistic

For a series, a forecaster and a day t, the e-value is e_t = (D^occ_t + D^mag_t) / (2n) with n = 365, where D^occ_t and D^mag_t are the e-detector statistics of the occurrence head and of the magnitude head of Part D2, computed on the monitored period of the window. The null hypothesis for a series is that both heads are calibrated up to day t.

## Panels

- A panel is the set of series that have a window at the same origin t0 in `out/events/falsealarm/windows.csv` and complete trajectories for all six scenarios of Part F. Origins with at least 3 such series are used. The origin is the unit for the intervals below.
- For each panel, 1000 random instances are drawn. In an instance each series is shifted with probability 0.3 independently, and a shifted series takes one of the five shifts of Part F (counts times 1.5, doubled, halved, 30% of positive days set to zero, 30% of zero weekdays set to one) with equal probability. The shift starts on monitored day 181. The trajectories are those of Part F, regenerated by `application/15_panel_trajectories.R` with the same injection seeds. The random draws use seed 20261112.
- Forecasters: F0 and F3 as in Part E.

## Rules

On each day, with M the number of series in the panel and alpha = 0.05:
- e-BH: sort the e-values in decreasing order, let k be the largest rank with e_(k) at least M / (alpha k), and flag the k series with the largest e-values (none if there is no such rank).
- Bonferroni: flag a series if its e-value is at least M / alpha.
- Uncorrected: flag a series if its e-value is at least 1 / alpha.

## Outcomes

- At the first day on which a rule flags at least one series (the first rejection time): the false discovery proportion, counting a flagged series as a false discovery if it is not shifted on that day (a shifted series is shifted only from day 181), the number of true discoveries, and the proportion of instances in which the rule flags anything in the window.
- At day 365: the false discovery proportion, and the power, the proportion of shifted series that are flagged.
- Averages over the instances, then over the origins with equal weight. Intervals are 95% percentile intervals from a bootstrap that resamples origins (2000 resamples, seed 20261113). Windows at nearby origins overlap in time, and the intervals ignore this dependence, which is stated in the report.

## Decision rule

For each forecaster, e-BH is reported as controlling the false discovery proportion if its estimate at the first rejection time is at most 0.05. It is reported as more powerful than Bonferroni if its power at day 365 is larger. The estimates for the uncorrected rule are reported for comparison. If the estimate for e-BH exceeds 0.05, the result is reported as a failure of control on real series, with the numbers.

## Not included

Other panel sizes, other shift probabilities, other forecasters, other rules, the whole forecast statistic, new events. Each requires a new protocol.
